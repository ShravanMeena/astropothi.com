# 02 · Product spec

## The core loop (must be under 60 seconds)

```
Login (phone+OTP)
  → [first time only] set up branding: photo, name, shop, phone, WhatsApp, logo, tagline
  → New Report
      → pick report type          (Premium Kundali / Dosh / Milan / Varshaphal / ...)
      → enter client birth details (name, dob, tob, place-autocomplete, gender, language)
      → pick a DESIGN             (visual thumbnail grid, 7 themes)
      → Generate                  (−N credits, 15–25s, progress screen)
  → Preview (page-flip) → Download PDF │ Share on WhatsApp │ Print
  → Client auto-saved to his Client Book
```

Everything else in the product exists to make him come back and do this again.

## 1. Branding profile (the white label)

One profile per account. Locked to the account; **max 2 changes per quarter, every change
logged** — this is both the anti-arbitrage control and an RBI closed-PPI requirement
(see [05-legal.md](05-legal.md)).

| Field | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Photo (portrait) | Cover, back page |
| Display name + honorific (Pt. / Acharya / Jyotishacharya) | Cover, every page footer |
| Shop / institution name | Cover |
| Phone, WhatsApp, email | Footer, back page CTA |
| Address | Back page |
| Logo (PNG/JPG; SVG auto-rasterised) | Cover, footer mark |
| Tagline / blessing line | Cover |
| Signature image | Back page |
| Preferred chart style | North / South / East / Bengali |
| Default language | en / hi (+ regional as they ship) |

**Zero Pothi branding on the PDF** — no footer link, no watermark, no "generated by".
The single exception is the non-removable legal disclaimer block (see 05-legal.md), which
is required and cannot be suppressed by the pandit's settings.

**Already built upstream:** `engine/reporting/branding.js` has `DEFAULT_BRANDING`,
`mergeBranding(overrides)` and `loadLogoBuffer()` with remote-URL fetch and SVG fallback.
We widen the shape (photo, shopName, whatsapp, tagline, signature, honorific) and swap the
Devpunya defaults for a neutral set.

## 2. The design system — the actual differentiator

Nobody in this market sells *design*. AstroSage Dhruv gives a name in a footer. Leostar
output looks like 2004. This is where a pandit's eyes light up, and it is why he pays us
rather than printing from free software.

**Seven launch themes.** Same content, different visual identity. He picks per report, so
he can hand a wedding family a red-gold book and a corporate client a minimal white one.

| id | Name | Palette | Cover | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kesari` | केसरी Kesari | Saffron / cream / brown ink | Arch border, marigold corners | Default. Traditional, safe |
| `vivah` | विवाह Vivah | Red / gold / maroon | Kalash + toran, foil-look gold | **Milan & wedding** — highest-value use |
| `shwet` | श्वेत Shwet | White / charcoal / one accent | Minimal, huge type, no ornament | Young urban clients, corporate |
| `neelkanth` | नीलकंठ Neelkanth | Deep indigo / silver / star-field | Night sky, constellation chart | Modern "astro-tech" look |
| `haritam` | हरितम् Haritam | Forest green / gold / ivory | Tulsi-leaf border | Ayurveda/health-leaning practices, Vaishnav |
| `swarn` | स्वर्ण Swarn | Black / gold / off-white | Black cover, gold foil emboss | The premium SKU he charges ₹1,100 for |
| `pothi` | पोथी Pothi | Parchment / sepia / red rubric | Manuscript leaf, string-tie art, Devanagari rubrics | The signature look. Sells "authenticity" |

Design rules for all themes:
- Devanagari and Latin must be **separately** type-set; do not scale one font to fit both.
  Hindi type at the same point size reads smaller — bump Devanagari 1.5–2pt and increase leading.
- Charts (North/South/East) inherit theme line/fill tokens, never hardcoded.
- Every theme must survive **black-and-white laser printing** — a large share of pandits print
  on a cheap mono printer. Test every theme at 1-bit before shipping it.
- Cover must look good as a **WhatsApp thumbnail** (first frame of the PDF preview). This is
  the actual sales surface for his client.

### How themes are implemented

**Do not fork the renderer.** Today `render-report-pdfkit.js` is 3,129 lines with 68
hardcoded hex colours; `render-dosha-pdfkit.js` 667 lines / 19 colours; the other three
16–19 each. Extract a token object and thread it as `T`:

```
engine/reporting/themes/
  index.js        registry + getTheme(id) + validateTheme()
  _base.js        every token with a default — themes are partial overrides
  kesari.js  vivah.js  shwet.js  neelkanth.js  haritam.js  swarn.js  pothi.js
  art/            per-theme border/corner/rule PNG+vector assets
```

Token surface: `palette{ink,inkSoft,accent,accentSoft,rule,tableHead,tableAlt,chartLine,chartFill,coverBg}`,
`fonts{headingEn,bodyEn,headingHi,bodyHi,sizes{}}`, `cover{layout,art,showPhoto,showLogo}`,
`page{border,watermark,footer,margins}`, `chart{cell,lineWidth,signLabel}`, `divider`, `bullet`.

Refactor order (pilot small, monster last): dosha → horoscope → varshaphal → laalkitab →
`render-report-pdfkit.js` with a visual-diff harness.

**Acceptance:** chart 5 rendered in 7 themes × 2 languages = 14 PDFs; byte-different,
content-identical (assert on extracted text equality).

## 4. Earnings dashboard (the retention mechanic)

He sets his own selling price per report type once. Then the home screen is:

```
      इस महीने आपकी कमाई
        ₹ 39,600
   132 रिपोर्ट  ·  लागत ₹528  ·  75× वापसी

   [ ▁▃▅▂▇▆█ ]  last 30 days
   368 credits left  ·  ~92 more Premium Kundalis     [ Recharge ]
```

Plus: most-sold report type, best month, all-time earned, "you saved N hours of typing".
This is a *behavioural* feature, not analytics — it is the reason he opens the app on a slow
day and the reason he re-buys. It also gives sales a screenshot to forward into WhatsApp groups.

**Honesty rule:** it is labelled "अनुमानित कमाई / estimated earnings, based on the price you
set" — never presented as verified revenue.

## 5. Client Book (digital vahi) — the annuity

Every generated report auto-saves the client: name, birth details, phone, which reports were
made, when. Searchable. This is genuinely valuable to him — Haridwar pandas maintain *vahis*,
genealogical registers going back 20+ generations, and are already digitising them. **The vahi
IS a customer database. Pitch: "turn your vahi into ₹250 reports."**

**The mechanic nobody exploits: automatic Varshaphal birthday reminders.** Every client needs
a new annual chart every year. On the client's birthday we push him a notification with a
one-tap "generate this year's Varshaphal and send". Converts a one-time buyer into recurring
income for him and recurring credit burn for us.

Also: re-download any past report free (no re-charge) for 12 months; regenerate with a
different theme costs 1 credit.

## 6. Delivery

- **WhatsApp share is the primary action**, not download. Deep-link `wa.me` with a short
  link to the PDF, prefilled Hindi message with his name. He sends from *his* number — so
  it costs us ₹0 in WABA fees and the client sees it come from their pandit.
- Short links `pothi.in/r/<8-char>` — no login for the client, expiring in 90 days,
  no Pothi branding on the landing page beyond the required disclaimer.
- Download PDF, print-optimised variant (bleed/margins, mono-safe).
- Phase 5: **print fulfilment via a POD partner** — 40 credits + a flat ₹399–499 fulfilment
  fee, resells at ₹1,500–3,000. Partner, never in-house; do not turn a zero-marginal-cost
  software company into a logistics company.

## 7. Deliberately NOT in v1

- Consultation marketplace / lead-gen back to us. **This directly conflicts with the
  white-label promise.** The moment he sees us acquire his customer, he leaves and tells the
  WhatsApp group. Revisit never, or much later, and only opt-in.
- AI chat / "ask the kundli". Nice demo, not why he pays.
- Free unlimited tier. Dukaan converted 0.06%.
- Auto-renewing subscription. UPI mandate failure + he resents paying in a dry month.
- Revenue share on his resale. He charges ₹300 cash and reports ₹100. Unenforceable.
- 8 languages. Ship **en + hi** properly; the validator claims 8 but only en/hi have real
  content packs. Add Marathi and Telugu next (Leostar taxes multilingual by 40% — undercut that).
