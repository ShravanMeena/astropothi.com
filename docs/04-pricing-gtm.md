# 04 · Pricing, unit economics and go-to-market

## Credit ladder

| Tier | Price (incl. GST) | Credits | ₹/credit | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trial | **₹0** | **10**, fully white-labelled | — | Get him to *sell one report and pocket ₹250.* That single event is activation |
| Chakhna | **₹249** | 40 | ₹6.20 | Under the ₹499 Vyapar psychological ceiling |
| **Standard** | **₹2,360** (₹2,000 + 18%) | **500** | **₹4.00** | **Hero SKU** |
| Purohit | ₹8,850 | 2,500 | ₹3.00 | Tirth-town / high-volume tier |
| Sangh / Institute | negotiated | 25,000+ | ₹2.00–2.50 | ICAS chapter, IVA, distributor |

**Quote GST-inclusive.** Most pandits are under the ₹20 lakh registration threshold, cannot
claim ITC, and our 18% is a dead ₹360 cost to them. Never surprise them at checkout.

**Credit-weighting, not flat per-report** (see [02-product.md](02-product.md)): a 64-chapter
Premium Kundali costs 5 credits, a Dosh report 2, Basic 1. Protects margin.

**Expiry:** purchased credits **12 months** (matches the RBI 1-year PPI benchmark, so a
consumer forum has nothing to grip). **Rollover on repurchase** — buying a new pack extends
all unexpired credits by another 12 months, which converts expiry anxiety into a purchase
trigger. Promotional credits 30 days, clearly labelled as such. Warn at 30/7/1 day.
Precedent: Astrotalk's own T&Cs already split 14-day promotional from 1-year purchased credits.

**No auto-renewing subscription.** UPI mandate failure rates are real and a pandit resents
being charged in a dry month. Offer a subscription later *alongside* packs, never instead.

## Unit economics

| | |
|---|---|
| Standard pack, ex-GST | ₹2,000 |
| Marginal cost (compute, S3, bandwidth, PG fee ~2%) | ~₹250 |
| **Contribution** | **~₹1,750 (87%)** |
| Annual logo churn | **35%** (matches the 3y2m average astrologer-business lifespan) |
| Packs/year, retained account | 1.8 |
| **3-year LTV** | **~₹6,900** |
| Affordable CAC at 3:1 | ₹2,300 |
| **Target CAC** | **≤ ₹600, payback < 3 months** |

**Breakage:** model **30–60% in cohort 1**, not the 6–10% SaaS benchmark. A 500-credit pack
takes a 20-reports-a-month pandit 25 months to exhaust; most churn first. Treat
credits-consumed-per-month-per-cohort as a **retention** metric, not a finance one —
*breakage from abandonment is churn wearing a costume.*

**Channel CAC estimates:**

| Channel | CAC |
|---|---|
| ICAS chapter partnership | ₹100–300 |
| Institute enrolment bundle | ₹150–400 |
| Pandit referral | ₹300–400 |
| Legacy software dealer conversion | ₹400–600 |
| CSC / Spice Money agent | ₹400–600 |
| Tirth-town feet-on-street | ₹500–900 |
| **Paid performance marketing** | **₹1,500–3,000 — do not** |

## Go-to-market: this is a list-and-territory motion, not performance marketing

The buyer is **enumerable**. That is the entire structural advantage over consumer faith-tech
(Sri Mandir burns 44% of opex on ads; VAMA 40%). We should spend near zero on ads in year one.

archakas who don't sell kundlis.

### Channels that already own the last mile

We may not need a pandit-only sales force at all:

| Network | Scale |

A CSC VLE or Spice Money Adhikari *is* the "local person who signs up 50 pandits" — already
onboarded, KYC'd, commission-trained, paid same-day. And **sell scratch-card credit vouchers
through samagri distributors at 15–20% trade margin, exactly like prepaid recharge.**

**Legacy software dealers** are an underrated list: a dealer who sold a ₹29,999 LeoStar three
years ago has a customer now out of support. Offer ₹400–600 per pandit converted, recurring on
re-orders. Dealer terms are unpublished — mystery-shop Astro-Vision at +91 6366920690.

**Influencers: target *teaching* channels, not prediction channels** — their audience *is* our
ICP. Astro Arun Pandit ~3M subs / 450M views (sells astrology courses), KRSchannel 614K,
Maneeza Ahuja 427K. Test a mid-tier 400–650K channel with unique coupon codes before touching 3M.

### Referral / agent ladder (mirrors recharge distribution)

- **Pandit referrer:** ₹200 credit + ₹200 cash, **paid on the referee's second pack** (not the
  first — that's how BharatPe avoided farming).
- **"Jyotish Mitra" local agent:** 20% of first pack + 10% lifetime.
- **District distributor:** 5% override + territory exclusivity.

## Phase 1 (0–90 days), zero paid ads

1. **Trimbakeshwar** (~150, public phone list) — door to door. Target 30 paying.
2. **Prayagwal Mahasabha** (1,884) — one negotiation with the Mahasabha.
3. **One ICAS chapter cluster** — Secunderabad or UP (9 chapters each).

Instrument three numbers and nothing else:
- **first-sale-within-7-days %** (the activation event — did he actually sell one?)
- **reports per pandit per month**
- **second-pack rate at 60 days**

## What to avoid, with the evidence

- **Free unlimited tier** — Dukaan: 3.5M merchants, 0.06% paid.
- **Performance marketing** — OkCredit: ₹157 cr of CAC → ₹23 cr revenue five years later.
- **Positioning as "software"** — Vyapar proves the ceiling for that word is ₹499/yr. We sell
  *reports* and *earnings*.
- **Counting registered pandits as a KPI** — DevDham signed 2,000 pandits across 18 states,
  raised $1.59M, and shut down.
- **Chasing salaried temple archakas.**
- **Ignoring that 43% of SMB losses happen in the first quarter post-purchase** — the first
  30 days of hand-holding is the product.

## Leakage risks and controls

1. **PDF reuse/editing** — *less bad than it looks*: rashi, lagna, all twelve houses, the dasha
   timeline and every dosh change completely with birth data, so a reused report is visibly
   wrong. Structural fix: make individualised content (charts, dasha tables, dated ranges)
   dominate the page count. Then raster the identity fields, print a visible Report ID +
   timestamp, and embed an invisible per-report fingerprint in metadata and chart geometry.
   Watermarking doesn't prevent piracy — it makes leaks **traceable**.
2. **Credit resale — highest damage.** *The tell is branding diversity*: a reselling pandit must
   emit reports under other people's names and logos. **Lock branding to the account, cap
   changes per quarter, log every change, alert on any account emitting under >2 profiles.**
   One day of engineering, and it also preserves the closed-loop PPI exemption. Then remove the
   incentive: ship a legitimate multi-seat plan.
3. **Account sharing** — device fingerprint, 2-session cap, OTP on new device. Shops share
   because we didn't sell them a multi-seat option.
4. **Corpus theft** — the ephemeris math isn't protectable; the **interpretation corpus is**
   (Copyright Act 1957). Register it. Per-tenant lexical variants so a leak identifies its source.
5. **Free-credit farming** — keep free credits at 10, phone + device dedupe, and tie the credits
   to an *activation action* (upload your logo) rather than to signup.
6. **Refunds/chargebacks** — follow Clickastro: no refund on generated reports. Unused-credit
   refund only within 7 days. State it **on the checkout page**, not just in the T&Cs.
