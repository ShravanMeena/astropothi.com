## Bottom line

The model is real, already attempted, and **still open — because nobody has executed
distribution.** The direct competitor doing exactly this idea has 1,000 Play Store
downloads. The category incumbent gives a *footer*, not a brand, and caps you at 100
reports/month. Wholesale white-label PDF costs ₹1.50–₹5/report from an API, so ₹4 is
defensible only because **we own the engine** — if we resold Prokerala at ₹62/PDF the
model would not exist.

Our real competitor is not a ₹999 subscription. It is **₹0 (Jagannatha Hora) plus a printer.**

## 1. Competitors

### Doing exactly this

**Jastro** — [jastro.in](https://jastro.in/) · Jagannath Astrology Pvt Ltd, Meerut · incubated by TIDES, IIT Roorkee.
Credit-based, *"fully branded with your photo, name, address and contact details on every
page"*, WhatsApp share, a dedicated **printer/print-shop dashboard** (a channel we should
not under-weight), hardcover print fulfilment 7–10 days. English + Hindi only.
**1K+ downloads, 28 reviews** ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jastro.kundlisoftware)).
→ *They validated the thesis and failed distribution. Strongest evidence the wedge is open.*

**GrahAI** — [grahai.com/practitioner](https://www.grahai.com/practitioner) · **₹24,999/yr**
(list ₹49,999) for 150 reports + 50 milan ≈ **₹167/report**; recharge ₹6,999/50. 9 languages,
credits never expire, *"No GrahAI marks, no GrahAI links."* Claims only **3,114 charts cast**.
→ *Same product, 40x our price, negligible traction.*

### The incumbent to beat

**AstroSage Dhruv Astro** — [astrosage.com/dhruv](https://www.astrosage.com/dhruv/) ·
**₹999/mo · ₹9,999/yr** · *"add your Name, Address & Contact Details on every page"* —
that is a **footer. No logo, no photo, no designed cover.** Hard cap **100 horoscopes/month**.
200–500pp colour, 9 languages, North/South/East styles, Parashari + Jaimini + KP + Lal Kitab
+ Tajik + Western + Numerology. **Only 4 reviews on Techjockey** despite AstroSage's 80M
consumer downloads → near-zero B2B mindshare.
→ *Our attack: design, photo-on-cover, no monthly cap, ₹4 not ₹999/mo.*

### The rev-share trap

|---|---|---|---|

guidance is *"INR 750 to INR 10 depending on the type of report"*
([source](https://www.indianastrologysoftware.com/professional/hindi-kundli-software-with-remedies.php)).
Independent validation of the ₹100–500 resale assumption, from the incumbent.

## 3. The buyer

### Honest TAM — reject the folklore

The "20 lakh astrologers" figure has no traceable source. What is verifiable:

| Layer | Count | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AstroSage "registered astrologers" (near-zero bar) | 700,000+ | [AstroSage](https://www.astrosage.com/about-us.asp) |
| Astrotalk vetted professionals | **45,634** | [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-astrotalk-seeks-unicorn-valuation-in-new-round-9485023) |
| **Smartphone + running a pro astrology work app** | **~142,000** | [AndroidRank](https://androidrank.org/application/only_for_astrologer_login/com.astrotalk.astrologer.app) ← best proxy |
| ICAS credentialled members | **21,000** across 60 chapters | [icasindia.org](https://www.icasindia.org/) |
| Astrologer businesses on Google Maps | **20,961** | [Rentech Digital](https://rentechdigital.com/smartscraper/business-report-details/list-of-astrologers-in-india) |

**Realistic SAM: 50,000–150,000 accounts. At 1.8 packs/yr × ₹2,000 that is a ₹20–60 crore
ceiling on the credit-pack line alone.** Calibrate expectations: **AstroSage — the company
that owns astrologer software — is a ₹60 crore business.** Selling *to* astrologers is a
fraction of selling *through* them. (Astrotalk, which sells through them, did ₹1,182 cr FY25.)

Google-Maps segment detail: **94.05% single-owner · 64% no website · only 11% have an email
· average business lifespan 3 years 2 months.** Top states: TN 2,654 · MH 2,596 · **UP 2,296**
· WB 1,655 · KA 1,313. **Email is not a channel. Phone + WhatsApp is the only channel.**

### Segments, ranked

**Tier A — buy immediately**
1. **Matchmaking / gun-milan pandits.** ~10M Indian weddings/yr, ~90% arranged; each match =
   2 kundlis + 1 milan; he charges ₹1,500–8,000. **60% of Astrotalk revenue is marriage-related.**
   *Marriage is the money — and we don't have a milan engine yet.*
2. **Marriage bureaus / matrimonial brokers.** 721 Hindu matrimonial bureaus in Delhi alone
   (Justdial), 667 Maharashtra, 460 WB → realistically **10,000–25,000 small bureaus.**
   Highest reports-per-account of any segment, already SaaS-conditioned. The big matrimony
   players are locked up (BharatMatrimony → Astro-Vision, Shaadi.com → AstrologyAPI); the
   small-bureau tier is not.
3. **Astrotalk / Astroyogi rejects.** Astrotalk's acceptance rate is **~5%**, adding ~200/week
   → **~4,000 rejections/week.** AstroSage's assessment passes 5,000 of 700,000 (**0.7%**).
   These people just paid ₹25,000–75,000 for a diploma, own a smartphone, want income, and
   have been told no.

**Tier B** — astrology shop owners (~21,000, 94% single-owner, already selling printouts from
2008 software: direct swap) · platform long-tail astrologers (reports already sell at ₹300–₹800
on Astrotalk; selling the same off-platform with zero commission is an arbitrage they grasp
in one sentence).

**Tier C** — temple pandits (648,907 temples but Google Maps returns only **1,368 "priests"**
nationally; they do *ritual*, not chart-reading — deprioritise except family purohits who also
do jataka/milan) · YouTube/Instagram astrologers (a *channel*, not volume) · jyotish institutes
(ICAS 21,000 · **IVA Indore claims 14,000+ students** · BVB Delhi — one institute head puts
200 students on the product).

### Their actual economics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Astrotalk astrologer payouts FY25 | **>₹511 cr** |
| Listed professionals | 45,634 |
| Mean payout | **~₹1.12 lakh/yr ≈ ₹9,300/mo** |
| Share actually active | ~half → **~₹18,600/mo per active** |
| Highest single monthly payout | ₹15,00,000 |

Brutally power-law. Astrotalk's own case study describes a pre-platform astrologer earning
**₹400–600/day offline with ~50 regular customers**, asking for a ₹15,000/month salary.
**That is the real economic condition of the typical Indian astrologer: ₹12,000–18,000/month.**
Our ₹2,000 pack is 4–5 days of gross income for 500 sellable units.

Small-temple priests earn **₹500–₹4,000/month**. A ₹2,000 pack is half a month's income for a
village pujari and a rounding error for a Trimbakeshwar purohit. **Do not treat "pandit" as one segment.**

### Digital literacy / payments
**9 of every 10 new Indian internet users are Indic-language users** (234M Indic vs 175M
English, Google–KPMG). **UPI is solved** — 90M+ active merchants, 709M QR codes; "doesn't
trust online payment" is a dead objection for a ₹2,000 UPI transaction. **Recurring mandates
are still a live blocker → prepaid packs, never auto-renewing subscriptions.** Age is bimodal:
a senior offline Windows cohort (50+) who will not switch, and a mobile-first Astrotalk-aspirant
cohort (22–35) who will. **Sell to the second.**

### Objections we will actually hear
1. **"Software is free — I have Jagannatha Hora."** Strongest one. → *We don't sell calculation. We sell a branded 40-page PDF on your client's WhatsApp in 20 seconds.*
2. **"Which ayanamsa? Your calculations are wrong."** Real and technical → Swiss Ephemeris + a published calculation-methodology page.
3. **"This replaces me."** → *It doesn't do the reading. It does the typing.*
4. **"Support is a scam."** Verbatim Future Point review: *"Before buying software they talk a lot of things, then after buying they ask for the charge of everything."* → **Free Hindi WhatsApp support is a differentiator.**
5. **"₹2,000 is suspiciously cheap"** — the market is trained on ₹20,000–30,000. Trust-transfer (association endorsement, known guru, local dealer) matters more than price messaging.

## 4. What Indian B2B SaaS to low-tech merchants taught us

**Khatabook — the cautionary tale.** 50M downloads, 10M MAU, $600M valuation.
**FY24: ₹102.7 cr revenue, ₹116.24 cr loss.** The quote that matters
([A Junior VC](https://www.ajuniorvc.com/bharat-startup-kirana-jio-b2b-housing-khatabook)):
> *"Most business owners in India do not value technology, primarily because the substitute labour is cheap... When you could pay your brother zero to manage your books, why would you pay Khatabook anything?"*

**OkCredit.** FY20 expenditure exploded **86x to ₹157.5 cr**, mostly acquisition. FY25
revenue: **₹23.3 cr.** ₹157 cr of CAC produced a ₹23 cr business five years later.

**Dukaan.** 3.5M merchants, **~2,000 on the premium plan = 0.06% paid conversion.**

**BharatPe — the CAC benchmark.** **₹200–250 CAC** via 10,000+ feet-on-street agents driving
~70% of signups; referral bounty up to ₹600/merchant paid across 3 months of activity.

**Vyapar — the counter-example proving they DO pay.** 1 crore users → **~1 lakh paying = 1%**
at **₹499/year**; FY24 ₹50.3 cr (+67%). It works because it is tied to a legally-forced,
money-touching job (GST invoices), charges tiny, and grew organically.
**₹499/yr is the psychological ceiling for the word "software" in this segment — which is
exactly why we sell "reports", not "software".**

**Faith-tech field precedent.** Sri Mandir: $20M Series C, 4 cr downloads — but **FY25 ad
spend ₹51.9 cr = 44% of expenses** on ₹69.6 cr revenue. VAMA: ₹19.66 cr revenue, marketing
40% of expenses. **DevDham: built 500+ temples and 2,000 pandits across 18 states, raised
$1.59M, then shut down.**

**Three conclusions.** (1) Pandit rosters are cheap to assemble and worthless alone — supply
coverage is not a moat, repeat purchase is. (2) Consumer faith-tech burns 40–44% of opex on
ads; **we don't have to, because our buyer is enumerable — that is our structural cost
advantage.** (3) **Astrotalk (45,634 astrologers) is simultaneously our best partner and our
biggest risk.** Approach them early as a supplier.
